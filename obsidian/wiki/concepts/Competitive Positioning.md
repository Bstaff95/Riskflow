# Competitive Positioning

Riskflow should position against the user's current workflow, not against a
generic category label.

## Main Alternatives

- manual TradingView review;
- generic TradingView indicators;
- crypto screeners;
- paid Discord or Telegram groups;
- quant dashboards;
- automated trading bots.

## Riskflow Wedge

Riskflow should win by combining relative leadership, warning/reset context,
chart-readable grammar, and transparent evidence memory.

It should not compete in v1 as a live trading bot, hosted dashboard, or
guaranteed signal product.
