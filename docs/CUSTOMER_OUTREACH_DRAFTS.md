# Riskflow Customer Outreach Drafts

These are draft messages and interview assets for future user-approved
customer discovery. They are not approved to send.

## Guardrail

Do not send outreach, post publicly, use private customer data, or imply product
performance without explicit user approval in the active thread.

## Short Warm Outreach

Subject: quick question about your crypto chart workflow

```text
Hey <name>, I am working on a research tool for active crypto chart review.
I am not trying to pitch you yet. I am trying to understand how serious traders
decide which charts deserve attention and which moves are late or unsafe.

Would you be open to a 20 minute call where I ask about your current workflow?
```

## Short Cold Outreach

Subject: 20 minute question on chart triage

```text
Hey <name>, I am researching how active crypto traders triage high-beta charts.
I noticed you spend time around <specific market/context>.

I am trying to learn how people decide what to watch, what to ignore, and what
tools fail them. Would you be open to a 20 minute research call? No pitch.
```

## Value-First Ask

```text
I can send a small sample watchlist/relative-strength report first, then ask
for feedback on whether it would have saved time or changed what you opened.
The goal is to learn whether this workflow is useful, not to sell anything yet.
```

## Interview Opener

```text
I am going to ask about your current process, not demo a product first. If
something sounds useful later, I can show a sample. I am more interested in
what is painful or confusing in your real workflow.
```

## Follow-Up Questions

- What did you open first today and why?
- What did you ignore today and why?
- Which chart looked exciting but you distrusted it?
- What tool helped you most?
- What tool created noise?
- What would make a report worth receiving again?
- What would make you stop using it?

## Post-Call Capture

- exact pain language;
- current alternative;
- workflow screenshot or reconstructed flow;
- sample report reaction;
- WTP or repeat-use signal;
- objection;
- assumption changed.

Production effect: none.
