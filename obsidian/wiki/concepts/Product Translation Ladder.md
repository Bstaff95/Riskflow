# Product Translation Ladder

Use this ladder before turning research artifacts into product language.

1. Observation: what the chart or oscillator appears to show.
2. Measured event: the observation is encoded as a sidecar feature or event.
3. Historical evidence: event study, grammar search, or strict referee result.
4. Visual review: examples support the intended chart-facing interpretation.
5. Fresh/withheld validation: evidence survives the validation contract.
6. Product role: warning, permission, invalidation, reset, path, gradient, or
   archive role is allowed for review.
7. Promotion proposal: user approval is required before production language or
   behavior changes.

Skipping levels is not allowed. A specialist result can recommend the next
level, but cannot approve promotion by itself.
