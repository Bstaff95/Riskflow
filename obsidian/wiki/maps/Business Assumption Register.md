# Business Assumption Register

This register keeps business hypotheses visible so CEO mode does not confuse
internal research progress with market proof.

## Assumptions

| Assumption | Status | Evidence | Next Test | Kill Condition |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Active crypto traders have painful chart-triage overload. | open | internal thesis | customer interviews | target users do not recognize the pain |
| Warning/reset grammar is valuable before buy/sell claims. | open | lab evidence stronger for warnings | manual report demo | users only want direct calls |
| TradingView companion plus local scanner is the wedge. | open | product thesis | workflow review | users reject local workflow or indicator context |
| Manual reports can test value before software packaging. | open | startup strategy | sample report feedback | no repeat-use or WTP signal |
| Research caveats are acceptable if prioritization is useful. | open | unknown | demo with caveats | users find caveats too weak to trust |

## Update Rule

Update this register when evidence changes an assumption, not after every run.
Each promoted assumption needs a source note, artifact reference, or user
learning record.
