# Riskflow As A Business

This document defines the business operating thesis for Riskflow. It is
strategy memory, not product proof and not runtime authority.

## First Customer

Riskflow should initially serve a serious discretionary trader or researcher
who already studies high-beta crypto charts, especially meme coins, and wants a
better way to answer:

- Which chart is gaining relative leadership before the obvious breakout?
- Which strong-looking move is actually late, extended, or unsupported?
- Which reset is constructive versus just chop?
- Which historical analogs support or weaken the read?
- What should be watched next, and what should be ignored?

The first user is not a fully automated fund, a passive retail dashboard user,
or a black-box signal buyer. The first user is someone who wants sharper chart
judgment with evidence-backed relative context.

## First Painful Workflow

The painful workflow is manual chart triage:

1. Scan many high-beta charts.
2. Compare each chart against the relevant basket.
3. Notice compression, reset, reclaim, divergence, and warning patterns.
4. Decide whether the setup is early, late, invalid, or worth watching.
5. Remember what similar patterns did historically.

Riskflow's business wedge is to make that workflow faster, more consistent,
and more honest about evidence.

## Product Wedge

Riskflow should enter as a research-grade TradingView companion and local
scanner, not as a live trading bot or generic dashboard.

The wedge:

- relative leadership versus the right basket;
- compression and reset context;
- chart-facing grammar learned from reviewed oscillator behavior;
- evidence-backed watchlist and warning language;
- explicit archive/do-not-repeat memory.

The first commercial value is not "buy/sell calls." It is better prioritization
and interpretation: which charts deserve attention, which require evidence, and
which should be ignored.

## Business Model Hypotheses

These are hypotheses to test, not commitments:

- premium research tool for active crypto traders;
- private scanner plus TradingView indicator companion;
- research subscription built around relative-leadership watchlists and
  setup-quality notes;
- eventually, a broader capital-flow research engine across crypto sectors,
  equities, and narratives.

Do not build licensing, billing, dashboards, or a hosted service in v1 unless
the local research lab proves a user-facing edge worth packaging.

## Forbidden Claims

Until fresh/withheld validation, visual review, and promotion gates support
stronger language, Riskflow must not claim:

- guaranteed alpha;
- automated trade recommendations;
- validated buy/sell signals;
- production-ready probability forecasts;
- promotion-ready product language from Obsidian notes alone;
- broad market coverage beyond tested local data.

## CEO Business Duties

CEO mode should continuously ask:

- Does this make Riskflow better at the first painful workflow?
- Which user-facing decision gets easier?
- What evidence supports saying that?
- What language is still forbidden?
- Which research lane is starved or overfunded?
- Which blocker requires user approval, fresh data, or archive?
- What would a fresh session need to know to continue?
