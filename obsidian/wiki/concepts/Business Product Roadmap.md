# Business Product Roadmap

The business roadmap converts lab progress into market-facing gates.

## Gates

1. Internal research lab.
2. Manual value test.
3. Design partner loop.
4. Beta package.
5. Productization decision.

Hosted dashboards, billing, auth, and automated delivery belong after manual
value and retention evidence, not before.
