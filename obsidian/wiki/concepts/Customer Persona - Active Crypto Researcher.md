# Customer Persona - Active Crypto Researcher

This persona is a hypothesis for Riskflow's first user. It should be refined
through [[Customer Discovery Log]] evidence.

## Profile

An active discretionary crypto trader or researcher who:

- reviews many high-beta charts;
- uses TradingView or similar charting tools;
- cares about relative strength versus a basket or narrative;
- wants early warning that a move is late, unsupported, or low quality;
- prefers explainable chart context over black-box trade calls.

## Pain

- too many charts to review manually;
- hard to compare every asset against the right basket;
- repeated false excitement from extended moves;
- limited memory of historical analogs;
- unclear distinction between constructive reset and chop.

## Desired Outcome

- fewer charts to open;
- clearer watch/avoid decisions;
- better reset and warning interpretation;
- evidence-backed reasons for attention;
- safer language about what remains unproven.

## Alternatives

- manual TradingView review;
- generic indicators;
- crypto screeners;
- Discord or Telegram curation;
- quant dashboards.
