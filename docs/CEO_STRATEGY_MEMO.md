# Riskflow CEO Strategy Memo

This memo is the current CEO-level strategy for Riskflow. It should change only
when evidence changes.

## Thesis

Riskflow can become a market-structure intelligence product by helping active
crypto traders and researchers understand relative leadership, warning
conditions, reset quality, and setup context faster than manual chart review.

The first business is not a trading bot, generic dashboard, or black-box signal.
The first business is a research-grade chart interpretation workflow.

## Wedge

Enter through the smallest painful workflow:

> Which high-beta crypto charts deserve attention, which are late or unsafe,
> and what evidence explains the read?

The wedge should be delivered through manual reports, local scanner output, and
TradingView-facing interpretation before hosted product infrastructure.

## Strategic Assets

- Pine-style oscillator and Python parity engine.
- Relative basket and compression research.
- Signal Grammar Lab and visual review loop.
- Evidence ladder and champion/challenger discipline.
- Obsidian research memory.
- CEO governance layer with approval, guardrail, replay, eval, and repair
  systems.

## Current Bet

Riskflow's best near-term product angle is warning and setup-quality
interpretation, not pure bullish entry prediction.

Reason:

- existing lab evidence is stronger for warning grammar than bullish triggers;
- active traders already have ways to get excited about charts;
- avoiding late, unsupported, or low-quality setups is a concrete pain;
- warning and reset language can be useful without claiming buy/sell authority.

## Non-Goals

- live trading bot;
- exchange execution;
- hosted dashboard;
- billing system;
- broad macro platform;
- ML prediction engine;
- production probability labels;
- default Pine behavior changes without approval.

## Strategic Risks

- Internal research quality improves while customer evidence stays weak.
- The product becomes too technical for discretionary traders.
- The system overfits meme-coin stale samples.
- Warning grammar is useful but hard to monetize.
- Local-data workflow blocks adoption.
- CEO mode confuses operating activity with business progress.

## Kill Criteria

Revisit the first-customer thesis if:

- five serious target users do not recognize the chart-triage pain;
- users prefer a simpler scanner or existing TradingView tools;
- warning/reset reports do not change any real watchlist decision;
- no target user can name a price or repeat-use condition;
- fresh/withheld validation weakens every chart-facing claim.

## Next Strategy Review

The next CEO strategy review should answer:

1. What customer evidence was added?
2. What product claim became safer or less safe?
3. What package is the smallest useful product?
4. Which research lane should receive less attention?
5. Which assumption should be killed, refined, or promoted?

Production effect: none.
