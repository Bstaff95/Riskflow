# Pricing And Packaging

Riskflow pricing is still hypothesis-level. CEO mode should test willingness to
pay manually before building billing, hosted accounts, or paid product surfaces.

## Package Hypotheses

- manual research note;
- TradingView companion;
- local scanner output;
- private beta cohort.

Each package must tie to a painful workflow and a renewal signal.

## Guardrail

Do not price or sell Riskflow as guaranteed alpha, automated trade calls, or
validated probability forecasts.
