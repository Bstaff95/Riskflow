# Riskflow Business Product Roadmap

This roadmap sits above the lab roadmap. It turns research progress into a
business sequence without authorizing production formula changes.

## Gate 0: Internal Research Lab

Status: active.

Required before leaving:

- reproducible scans and event studies;
- champion/challenger comparison;
- visual review for selected candidates;
- clear forbidden claims;
- customer-discovery plan.

## Gate 1: Manual Value Test

Goal: prove one user-facing output is useful before productizing.

Deliverables:

- one manual watchlist or chart-triage report format;
- one TradingView companion interpretation guide;
- one local scanner report example;
- customer interview guide;
- assumption register.

Pass condition:

- at least one target user says which decision became easier or what would make
  the output worth repeated use.

## Gate 2: Design Partner Loop

Goal: learn from 3 to 5 target users.

Deliverables:

- repeated report cadence;
- user feedback log;
- objection register;
- packaging test;
- board-style weekly review.

Pass condition:

- repeated usage or explicit willingness-to-pay signal.

## Gate 3: Beta Package

Goal: package the smallest repeatable product.

Possible package:

- TradingView companion;
- local scanner export;
- weekly relative-leadership and warning report;
- private walkthrough.

Pass condition:

- users keep using it across changing market weeks;
- product language remains evidence-safe;
- lab evidence supports the claims being made.

## Gate 4: Productization Decision

Only after Gates 1 to 3 should Riskflow consider:

- hosted dashboard;
- billing;
- auth;
- automated report delivery;
- broader asset coverage;
- customer-facing docs beyond research disclaimers.

## CEO Roadmap Rules

- Do the least engineering that tests the riskiest business assumption.
- Prefer manual delivery until retention or willingness-to-pay evidence exists.
- Keep lab evidence, product language, and business claims separate.
- Never let a business roadmap gate override production approval gates.

Production effect: none.
