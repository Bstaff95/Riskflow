# CEO Board Reports Index

This index tracks board-style CEO reports and follow-through. It is continuity
memory for future sessions.

## Purpose

A board report should answer:

- what got better;
- what was falsified;
- what customer or product claim moved;
- what remains unsafe to say;
- what decision or approval is needed from the user;
- what the CEO mode will do next.
