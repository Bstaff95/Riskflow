# Business Metrics

Business metrics measure whether Riskflow is becoming useful to a market, not
whether the internal lab is busy.

## Track

- interviews completed;
- repeated pain;
- workflow observations;
- time saved or charts removed from review;
- willingness-to-pay signal;
- repeat usage;
- evidence-safe claim readiness.

## Do Not Count

- internal reports alone;
- agent activity alone;
- Obsidian notes without decision deltas;
- process-score improvement without user-facing value.

Source doc: `docs/BUSINESS_METRICS.md`

Production effect: none.
