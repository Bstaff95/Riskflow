# Riskflow Competitive Positioning

This document maps Riskflow against alternatives. It is strategy memory, not
market proof and not product authority.

## Positioning Thesis

Riskflow should not compete as "another indicator." It should compete as a
relative-leadership and warning-context workflow for active crypto chart
research.

The core position:

> Riskflow helps serious traders decide which high-beta charts deserve
> attention, which are late or unsupported, and what evidence explains the read.

## Alternatives

### Manual TradingView Review

- Strength: flexible, visual, familiar.
- Weakness: slow across many assets, hard to remember analogs, inconsistent
  relative comparisons.
- Riskflow wedge: faster triage plus relative basket context and evidence
  memory.

### Generic TradingView Indicators

- Strength: easy to install and visually familiar.
- Weakness: usually single-chart, absolute, and disconnected from tested
  historical context.
- Riskflow wedge: relative leadership, compression/reset context, and
  evidence-backed warning language.

### Crypto Screeners

- Strength: broad coverage and simple filters.
- Weakness: often momentum/volume sorted, not setup-quality aware.
- Riskflow wedge: rank attention by relative structure, not just recent move.

### Paid Discord Or Telegram Groups

- Strength: curation, narrative, social proof.
- Weakness: opaque process, incentives unclear, hard to audit.
- Riskflow wedge: transparent evidence and do-not-repeat memory.

### Quant Dashboards

- Strength: breadth, metrics, sometimes institutional polish.
- Weakness: can be too abstract for chart-native discretionary users.
- Riskflow wedge: translate numeric evidence back into chart-readable grammar.

### Fully Automated Trading Bots

- Strength: execution automation.
- Weakness: different buyer, high trust burden, high risk.
- Riskflow wedge: do not compete here in v1.

## Differentiators To Prove

- Better relative triage than manual chart scanning.
- Better warning/reset explanation than generic indicators.
- Better evidence traceability than social signal groups.
- More chart-readable than generic quant dashboards.
- Safer and more honest than automated-call products.

## Positioning Guardrails

Do not claim:

- guaranteed alpha;
- validated buy/sell signals;
- fully automated trade recommendations;
- broad market superiority;
- replacement for risk management.

Do claim only when evidence supports it:

- faster triage;
- clearer relative context;
- warning/reset interpretation;
- evidence-backed watchlist reasoning;
- transparent limitations.

## CEO Questions

- Which alternative is the user using today?
- What does that alternative solve well?
- What pain remains after they use it?
- Which Riskflow output would they switch for?
- Which claim is safest to test manually?

Production effect: none.
